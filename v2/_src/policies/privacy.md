This policy explains what personal information Lowell Herb Co. ("Lowell", "we", "us") collects when you use lowellherbco.com and the Farm Store (the "Site"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. {{To confirm: the legal entity that operates the Site.}}

We don't sell cannabis or hemp online. Lowell pre-rolls are sold only by licensed dispensaries. When you buy from a dispensary, its own privacy policy applies, not this one.

## Information we collect

### Information you give us
- **Contact details:** your name, email address, phone number and mailing address.
- **Order details:** billing and shipping addresses, the goods you buy, and confirmation of payment. Card numbers go to our payment processor; we don't store them.
- **Account details:** if you create an account, your login and order history.
- **Messages:** what you write when you email us, sign up for updates, or leave a review.
- **Age confirmation:** the Site asks whether you're 21 or older. Your answer is stored only in your own browser.

### Information collected automatically
When you visit the Site, we and our service providers collect technical information: device and browser type, IP address, the pages you view, the links you click, and how you arrived. Cookies, pixels and similar tools collect it. See [Cookies and similar tools](#cookies-and-similar-tools).

### Information from others
We receive information from the companies that help us run the Site: our store platform (Shopify), payment processors, shipping carriers, and our email and text-message provider. {{To confirm: advertising and analytics partners on the new store.}}

## How we use your information
- To process and ship your orders, handle returns, and answer your questions.
- To send you emails or texts you've asked for, such as order updates, new goods and restock news. You can opt out at any time.
- To understand how the Site is used, and to improve it.
- To prevent fraud, keep the Site secure, and enforce our [Terms of Service](terms.html).
- To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

## Finding a store near you
The Find Lowell locator runs in your browser. We don't store the ZIP code or city you type. The store list is loaded from our own servers. The map is drawn with tiles from a third-party map provider, which receives your IP address the way any website you load does. {{To confirm: the map provider for the live site.}}

## Cookies and similar tools
We use cookies to keep the Site working (for example, your cart), to remember your preferences, and to measure how the Site is used. {{To confirm: whether the live store uses advertising cookies or pixels, and which.}}

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Site may not work without them. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information, as the law in your state requires.

## When we share information
We don't sell your personal information for money. We share it:
- **With service providers** who work for us: store hosting, payments, shipping, email and text messaging, customer service and analytics. They may use it only to provide their services to us.
- **With advertising partners**, where the law treats this as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out; see [Your privacy rights](#your-privacy-rights). {{To confirm: whether this applies on the new store; remove this item if not.}}
- **When the law requires it**, or to protect our rights, our customers, or the public.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger or a sale of the business, subject to this policy.
- **When you ask us to.**

## Email and text messages
We send marketing emails and texts only if you sign up. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Message and data rates may apply. Signing up for texts is never a condition of buying anything. We don't share your mobile number with other companies for their own marketing. Order and service messages may continue after you unsubscribe from marketing.

## Your privacy rights
Depending on the state you live in, you may have the right to:
- know what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy
- correct it, or ask us to delete it
- opt out of targeted advertising, and of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information
- appeal if we turn down your request

To make a request, email [admin@lowellherbco.com](mailto:admin@lowellherbco.com). We'll confirm your identity before acting on it, and we'll respond within the time the law allows. You can use an authorized agent; we'll ask for proof that the agent is acting for you. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

## Age requirement
The Site is for adults 21 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

## Security and retention
We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. We keep personal information for as long as we need it: to fulfil orders, provide service, meet legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Then we delete it or make it anonymous.

## Links to other sites
The Site links to Instagram, to dispensary menus and to press articles. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy doesn't cover them.

## Changes to this policy
We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you by email or with a notice on the Site.
